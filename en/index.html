HOW IT WORKS

Why compress "to an exact size"

Most compressors just "make it smaller", but many situations need it to be under an exact KB — ID photo uploads (many systems cap at 20–200KB), email attachments, government / forum forms. This tool takes a target KB and approaches it, locally.

Pick image & target

Drop an image, choose 20/50/100/200KB or type your own. The file only enters this page.

Approach locally

It compresses repeatedly in your browser toward your target KB — no server.

Download

Once it meets the size, download it. Close the tab and nothing is left behind.

FAQ

FAQ

How do I compress an image to under 100KB?
Upload an image, pick a target (e.g. 100KB) or type a KB value, click Compress. It approaches your target locally and won't exceed the KB you set.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. Everything happens locally in your browser; images are never uploaded. You can verify in the DevTools Network tab — no upload request.
Will quality drop after compression?
A very small target (like 20KB) lowers quality to hit the size; looser targets lose less. Best for hard size limits (ID photos / attachments).
Which formats? Any size limit?
JPG / PNG / WebP. Local processing means no upload cap, but very large images are bound by device memory and may be slow on phones.
